# Junie guidelines

All assistant guidance lives in `AGENTS.md` files. Before any task:

1. Read the root `AGENTS.md` (repo map, commands, healthcare data rules, and mandatory skill & workflow protocol).
2. Follow the Skill & Workflow Protocol: check available skills with `using-superpowers` and clarify workflow preferences (superpowers planning vs. direct execution) with the user.
3. Read the `AGENTS.md` of the area you are working in:
   - `packages/healthcare-ai-core/AGENTS.md`
   - `packages/lab-agent/AGENTS.md`
   - `services/AGENTS.md` (and `services/<name>/AGENTS.md` once a service exists)
